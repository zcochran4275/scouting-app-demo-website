    The label

  5. 5

Every section of the report

Each section answers a different coaching question, with findings built the same way.

Offensive

How they hurt you

  • Chance originsHow their attacks start: winning the ball, set pieces or build-up.
  • DribblingWhere they take players on, and whether it works.
  • Shooting and shot assistsWhere they shoot from and how the final ball arrives.
  • Progression and box entriesHow they move the ball forward and into your box.
  • Build-up tempoFast or patient, and which one pays off for them.

Defensive

Where you can hurt them

  • How they concedeThe routes that lead to chances and goals against them.
  • Defensive duelsWhere on the pitch they win and lose their duels.
  • PressingHow high and how hard they press, and what sets it off.
  • DisciplineCards and fouls, and who is at risk.

Team profile

How they run a game

  • Substitution patternsWhen they make changes, and how many at once.
  • Results by outcomeWhat looks different in the games they win and lose.
  • PenaltiesWho takes them, and how they have gone.

Matchups

Built for your team

Your team against theirs, both ways

  • Your attack vs their defenceWhere the way you create meets the way they concede.
  • Their attack vs your defenceThe mirror: where they can hurt you.
  • Player matchupsThe individual battles to win, from who actually faces whom.
  • Once subs come onHow the matchups change after their usual changes.
