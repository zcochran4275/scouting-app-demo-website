What it will include

01

Portal players and their D1 stats

Review portal entries and player stats together.

02

Playing style by position

Each player tagged with the role they play, like a ball-playing center back or a wide creator. Players who mix two roles show as hybrids.

03

Ratings by part of the game

Scores for each main part of the game, such as build-up, chance creation and defending, to compare players at a glance.

04

Credit for the actions that lead to goals

Include actions earlier in the attack alongside goals and assists.

05

Fit with your team

How well a player suits the way you play and who you are losing, so you can see who replaces a departing starter.

06

Search and compare

Filter by position, style and rating, then put players side by side.
