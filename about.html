Why it exists

Opponent prep at the college level means hours of film and stat sheets that someone still has to turn into a plan. Most staffs don't have a full-time analyst to do that for every opponent.

This tool does the reading for you. It goes through an opponent's full season, compares every tendency with the rest of D1, and writes out what makes them different in plain coaching language, with the stats, visuals and film moments behind each point.

Between a player's view and an analyst's, we know what's useful on matchday and what isn't. Every page is built to answer a question a coaching staff actually asks.
